paragraph this page is built around.

The argument, from T1's compensated/uncompensated diagram: inside the
compensated region, NTP or PTP subtracts out network delay, so time reads the
same everywhere. Outside it, delay is **not** compensated — and the pulse-per-
second line and the antenna feed line are the two variables that live there.

That split is why this topic exists separately from
[Acquiring Time](/timekeeping/acquiring/), and it is the reason
[your feed line matters](/timekeeping/antennas/feed-line/) far more than its
length suggests: it sits in the uncompensated region, where nothing corrects
for it but you.
