# About

If something isn't going as fast as it possibly can, I want to know why — and
then I want to fix it. That impulse is the only thread running through all of
this.

**Career 1.0** was compilers, operating systems, and network stacks — the layers
everything else stands on.

**Career 2.0** was messaging software startups and reliable multicast: getting
the same data to many places at once, quickly, without losing any of it.

**Career 3.0** was electronic trading and market structure research at Citadel
Securities, where the question of what "fast" means gets unusually precise.

I'm retired now, and researching the limits of practically precise timekeeping —
not the theoretical floor, but how close you can actually get with equipment you
can buy and run yourself.

This blog is where I write down what I find out. The name plays on "think
outside the box."
